VERDANT, GREEN, AND NATURAL

===

To be or not to be; that is the question... and yet, that is all the more the point;
If being is in nature, then is being inclined to reside alongside it? Or should being be itself alone? No. Why not let being be in nature and learn best how to be?

Nature VS nurture? But what of nature and nurture? Don't both work together?

"So when you met the new you..."

Let us discover, not just recover, progress; and in doing so, we shall come to nature...

"Were you scared? Were you cold?"

Let the sun shine and warm us up in our castles and balconies, though they may be frail.

"Were you kind?"

Let nature swarm. Let the bees create their honey. Let them build their castles in the sky, ever bright.

"Yeah, when you met the new you... did someone die inside?"
