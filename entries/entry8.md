OPENING POSSIBILITY

===

Open the door of possibility. Go ahead. What you mind find may be scary, but that's okay.

"You were the child who was made of glass."

The fear may chill you, but never let it freeze you.

"You carried a black heart passed down from your dad."

Everest was conquered not through frozen action, but through moving action,

"If somebody ... they'd tell you by now,"

and through the sacrifices of many who, having lived there for their life, guided others to the top.

"We all turn away when you're down."

Open possibility, but never forget those who helped you.

Be thankful. Be wise.
