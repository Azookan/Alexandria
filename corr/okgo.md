the third convo we had was something i can't remember too well. i locked the hazy record with something i can't remember either, but that's okay. i can dream. that's what kayla said.

i can dream.

isn't that wonderful? all those things, all the hurt, all my askesis - it may fade away when i dream. i long to be in that place of dreams.

i see the sun come up, and i dream of my little castle along the sands of a faraway kingdom. the sun's rays are like knights on horses, coming to warm up the land and rid it of the bandits: the bad elements. and i live every day as an adventure, using sword and shield and staff to guide my way to treasure. that's what i want to do. that's the dream i'll dream.

and yet i realize that all my lies, my fakeness, still had a bit of truth. i still like the better blue sky, and while i can never be as cheery about math in my average-ness, i still like the way the variables play together and how we create arguments like skyscrapers.

it's like fiction. fiction is a lie, but to create that lie, you need to have some understanding of reality. in that way, fiction is a sort of defeated mimesis, an imitation which is so blatantly fake and yet calls out for us to accept it. and i will accept that mimesis i made for myself - me, in a forest whose shrubs resemble the dye of my beret. and still, standing there, i taste the stone-fruit jam which enlivens the symbol of my lie.

i am a more free me.
